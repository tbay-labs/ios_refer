Who qualifies

Read this before you invite anyone. Rewards that break these rules are cancelled, and repeat attempts can suspend an account.

  • We verify that your friend has never used Tbay before. Every entry is checked against our records. A different email address does not make someone a new user. If the person you refer has held a Tbay account at any point, the entry is disqualified and no reward is paid to either side.
  • One reward per device, ever. If a device has already been used to claim a referral reward, a new account on that same device will not earn another one.
  • Creating several accounts on one device does not work. Extra accounts opened on the same device are ineligible, and all rewards linked to them are withdrawn.
  • You must submit your referral code on this page. Referrals made without registering your code here are not part of this campaign and earn no bonus.
  • Your friend must join on iPhone. The account has to be created on the iOS app. Android sign-ups are not part of this campaign.
  • ₦50,000 of trades must settle within 30 days of your friend creating their account. It can be one trade or several. The clock starts at sign-up, not at download. Cancelled, reversed or declined trades do not count towards the total.
  • You cannot refer yourself. Accounts sharing a device, bank account or verified identity with the inviter are ineligible.
  • Rewards are credited within 48 hours. Once your friend reaches ₦50,000 in settled trades and the entry passes verification, ₦2,000 reaches each Tbay wallet within 48 hours.

Questions

How many friends can I invite?

There is no fixed limit, but unusually high referral activity is reviewed manually before rewards are released. Genuine invites always get paid.

My friend already downloaded the app but has not entered a code. Can they still add it?

The code has to be entered during sign-up. If they have already created an account without it, that account cannot be linked to your referral afterwards.

Does the ₦50,000 have to be a single trade?

No. It is a running total. Your friend can reach ₦50,000 across as many trades as they want, as long as it all settles within 30 days of creating their account.

What if my friend does not reach ₦50,000 within 30 days?

The ₦2,000 iPhone bonus does not apply, and the window cannot be extended. The 30 days run from the day they created their Tbay account. Your standard referral rewards are separate from this campaign and follow their own terms.

I use Android. Can I still invite people?

Yes. Your own device does not matter for the bonus. Only your friend needs to join on iPhone.

How do you check whether my friend is really a new user?

Every entry is matched against our account records and device information before a reward is released. Signing up with a different email address does not create a new user in our records, so please only invite people who have genuinely never traded on Tbay.

Why was my reward not paid?

The most common reasons are a friend who turns out to be a returning user, a device that has already claimed a reward, a referral code that was never submitted on this page, or a friend who did not reach ₦50,000 inside the 30-day window. Contact support in the app and the team will tell you which rule applied.
